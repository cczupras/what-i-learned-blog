#2 Tech 💻

Cloning Myself: How I’m Using AI to Automate My Blogging Workflow

What if you could clone yourself to get more done? That’s exactly what I’ve been experimenting with. I’m not talking about a sci-fi movie plot; I’m talking about using AI to build a digital version of me that can handle my blogging workflow. It all started when I connected my Notion workspace to an MCP server, and from there, things got interesting.

The Setup: My Digital Ecosystem

My goal was to create a seamless flow from idea to published article. I started by organizing my projects in Notion and connecting them to Visual Studio Code. With GitHub Copilot, I can now analyze data, rewrite content, and edit my posts with incredible efficiency. For my blog, I’ve created a dedicated folder that syncs with my Notion database, giving the AI direct access to my notes and research.

Creating My Digital Twin

The real breakthrough came when I decided to create a persona—a digital version of myself. I’ve had great success with personas in my professional work, so why not for my personal blog? I’m building my own chat mode, an agent that represents my voice and writing style. This "digital me" can take my raw audio notes, which are often scattered and unpolished, and transform them into well-structured, engaging articles.

From Messy Audio to Polished Post

I often record my thoughts as audio notes, but they’re not always ready for publication. I jump between ideas, reflect on the past, and think ahead—all in a stream of consciousness that doesn’t make for a great reading experience. Instead of spending hours transcribing and editing, I can now feed these recordings directly to my AI agent. It summarizes the content, refines the language, and ensures the final article is something you’ll enjoy reading.

The Future is Automated

My vision is to have a fully automated workflow. I record an audio note, it gets transcribed and stored in my Notion database, and then my AI persona writes the article and sends it back to the right page. No more copy-pasting, no more manual formatting. This process not only saves me an incredible amount of time but also allows me to focus on what I love most: exploring new ideas and sharing them with you.

#3 Sports, Food & Health 🏋️🚴🥦

First time 100k in one day 🚵‍♂️

I you have read last weeks blog then you already know that I have joined Team Rynkeby Flensburg.
Yesterday a little Team tour was announced in the intranet calendar and I thought it would be a good idea to join and meet more of my Team members. The route was announced with 55km in our beautiful region. The start was about 24km far from my home and I thought it would be a good idea to take the bike also to get to the meeting point and after the tour back home.

My routes yesterday:

Thoughts on the distances

In total that would make more that a 100km in one day - a distance I have never ridden before. Well if you know me - I am always open for a good challenge - so I gave it a go. As you can see in the picture the time on bike was around \~ 4:30h yesterday. And I have to admit it was not too bad.
When cycling to Paris next year with the team we will most certainly spend the double amount of time on the bike each day. In average we are going to ride around 180km per day - so time to start spending more time in the saddle until next year July.

Refill water when possible

One learning I take is to refill my water bottles. I had around 1.5 liters with me and in the evening back home I felt I could have drunk a bit more fluid.
The only thing I feel aking a bit is my neck - not sure if that is because of the position on the bike which I am not that used to or if it was the little backpack I had with me that dragged a bit too much on the shoulders.

Key Learnings and Todo’s

  • Refill water in breaks when possible ❌
  • Bring enough food ✅
  • I had oat cookies with chocolate - good for sharing
  • little bananas
  • date and nuts bars
  • bought some backed goods from a bakery when arriving at the starting point of the tour
  • Use a different approach to store rain clothing and food ❌
  • may be I should invest in a frame bag for my gravel bike? ℹ️
