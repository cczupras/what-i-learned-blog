#2 Tech

A modern notebook - Googles NotebookLM

I want to discuss Google Notebook LM, a web application that's transforming how I approach new topics. It's like a smart notebook where you can add notes and drag in various sources such as webpages, emails, documents, and YouTube videos.
You can chat with a generative AI that understands your sources, allowing you to ask specific questions without it accessing external data. The amount of data you can add is vast.
One standout feature is the ability to create a podcast where AI speakers discuss your sources. It's best to have a separate notebook for each topic, adding relevant sources to each.
I use it as a learning and research tool, and for this blog, I import articles to ask specific questions and improve my writing.
Recently, I discovered the mind map feature, which automatically creates a visual map from your sources. Each node can be expanded to show summaries and source details.
An upcoming feature will allow Google to search and add relevant sources directly to your notebook. You can pin important answers as persistent notes and save mind maps for future reference.
I highly recommend trying Google Notebook LM for researching topics and extracting information from various sources. It's free, with a pro version available, though the free version is more than sufficient for most needs. Give it a try and share your experience!

How to Train an AI

Imagine teaching a pet a new trick. Training an AI is similar, except we're teaching a computer to learn something.
Step 1: Collect many examples
If you want to teach a computer to distinguish cats from dogs, you need many pictures of cats and dogs. The more examples, the better the AI will learn.
Step 2: Prepare the data
You need to prepare all the images so the computer can understand them. This means converting them into numbers, because computers only understand numbers.
Step 3: Choose a model
A model is like a recipe that tells the computer how to learn. There are many different types, just like there are different recipes for cakes.
Step 4: Training
Now you show the computer all your examples and tell it: "This is a cat" or "This is a dog." The computer tries to guess, and you tell it whether it's right or wrong. After many attempts, it gets better and better.
Step 5: Testing
When you think your AI has learned well, you show it new images it has never seen before to check if it can really distinguish cats from dogs.
Step 6: Improve
Usually, you need to adjust some things to make the AI even better. You give it more examples or change the model a little.
The great thing is: This is exactly how big AIs like me are trained too, just with much, much more data and on much larger computers!

About the Artist:
Papa Roach is a hard rock/nu-metal artist/band from Vacaville, California. Known for their intense and emotional songs, Papa Roach has been a significant force in the rock music scene, with notable achievements such as earning two Grammy nominations and selling over 20 million copies worldwide. The band, consisting of Jacoby Shaddix, Jerry Horton, Tobin Esperance, and Tony Palermo, has had a career marked by outstanding news and happenings, including the release of their triple-platinum debut album "Infest" and multiple chart-topping singles

Background of the Song:
"Leave a Light On" was released on April 8, 2022, and is part of the album "Ego Trip." The song is about the pain of watching someone suffer and offers a beacon of hope, emphasizing the importance of being there for someone going through a dark time. The lyrics highlight themes of loneliness, pain, and the need for emotional support. Interestingly, the song has resonated deeply with audiences, climbing to the top of Billboard's Mainstream Rock Airplay chart in February 2024, marking Papa Roach's 10th #1 hit on the tally. Additionally, the band has been donating the royalties from this song to the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention, demonstrating their commitment to mental health awareness
