#2 Tech

Interfaces in AL (Business Central)

I am working for a company (AKQUINET - IT-Solutions) which implements the Microsoft ERP system in all kinds of businesses. We are consulting our customers when introducing this system from scratch or help migrating from an older version or even a different platform. We also develop customisations for the specialized business processes of our customers.
Last year I started to help out another team and had a task to look into a proof-of-concept for a new requirement. Our customer has a DMS (Document Management System) and wants the new System to connect with it to store all documents like Invoices similar as they do in the old (current) system. I figured out this will be possible with the help of a product we have created last year. Basically a little App (Extension to the Business Central) that lets you setup Webservice Connections easily.
Based on that product I planned to create another Extension, a Connector for the DMS.

Both these Apps should work together as one and I would build them reusable for others. Read more…

So I was gathering some thoughts and put them on the drawing board to build a rough concept which I think could work. For me this is a process over some days. Normally I do not have big time slots during the day to focus on bigger tasks. I have quite some customers an colleagues that I meet during the day to look into various things - may it configuration, help with some implementations, checking erros or collect new requirements.
But that is not an issue, on the contrary it helps me develop the ideas. So I start with the basics and build the first small block by implementing a first setup table, then an action that executes some test code. Next would be fetching the documents that are uploaded in the ERP system by a generic configurable process. After collecting all the data it will be put in a processing table. And in the end it will be processed and send to the configured webservice interface of the DMS.
Putting together all these pieces sound quite straightforward but it is not. A lot of times I build a small part and then refactor it couple hours later. Because during commuting or walking outside I got a better idea how to build it easier and reusable. What I also do in between is browse the web for best practices in coding, design patterns or how to use certain features of the programming language. All to add 1% more to my knowledge.
But long story short you want to know where I use Interface. Let me first tell you what Interface in AL (programming language of Business Central) are:

AI interfaces in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central offer clear advantages. First, they increase consistency and stability by establishing clear contracts for implementation. Second, they promote code reusability, which accelerates development and reduces errors. And third, they enable better collaboration between different development teams through defined communication interfaces. In summary: stability, efficiency, and teamwork boost.

- thanks CoPilot for wrapping this up for me 😉 -

Doesn’t that sound great and like every developer wants and should use them? Well yes, but you need to understand where these are useful and you need to be sharp about the contract - you can’t change it afterwards - or you will break things.
I decided Interfaces will be a perfect match for my DMS Connector App as I have different input data and may be different output data based on the DMS system I want to connect to. I wanted to build the App in a way that it can handle different DMS systems. So it should be well prepared for extensibility right from the start.
To give you a little picture how that looks in my head here is a simple diagram:

As you can see the main setup in the middle connects everything and determines, by configuration, which interface implementation will be used during processing. It looks quite neat in code, is easy to read for other developers and most important it offers a clear structure for extension.

Neat and clean like I love it.
Let’s assume another team, another developer needs to connect to another DMS. Now they can grab a clone of the Repository, create a new branch and start working on a new Output Interface Handler. By contract they have to implement a set of given procedures, for example which setup page to open for the DMS specific configuration or which webservices to call in which order when sending the document.
The App is not finished yet, it is still in the beginning so you will most likely read about the progress in next weeks blog.

I will start this text with an excuse right away: I had too little time for Fitness training.
That is easy to say as I had a quite busy working week with commuting and traveling as well. My body was not feeling bad, instead after the long vacation it felt good - no real aches or discomfort. But I also felt the urge to move while I was a bit tired - so what to do?

Reflecting on the recording, my exercise routine was disrupted by work travel and various appointments. However, I have a plan to get back on track—I'll aim to exercise at least twice a week for 30-45 minutes using my garage gym. Since the recording I achieved to get into my home gym 2 times already doing some strength work. Today on sunday I’m enrolled in the 10:30 AM CrossFit Class which makes it to 3 times exercising this week. Didn’t I start with an excuse I had too little time? Well somehow I found the motivation and time this week. But what about the next weeks?
To make this possible throughout the next month or at best the whole year and on to let it become a habit, I need to find some time after work. One possibility to get more time in the evening is ingredient prepping, where you prepare ingredients for meals instead of the full meal, particularly on Sundays. This method will allow me to prepare ingredients in advance and mix-and-match them throughout the week, like having rice that can be paired with different toppings over several days.
The upcoming CrossFit Games Open will be particularly interesting since I've been primarily focused on cardio lately. This event, which takes place over three weekends in February and March, will be my 9th time participating—a streak I'm determined to maintain despite not being as fit as in previous years. While I haven't done much strength work recently, I'm eager to rebuild that foundation, particularly focusing on lower back strength and leg work. This will not only improve my overall fitness but also benefit my cycling performance, especially as I look forward to the upcoming bike season where I can enjoy long rides in Heart Rate Zone 2 and enjoy the nature.
