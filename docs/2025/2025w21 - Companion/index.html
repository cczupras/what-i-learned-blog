#2 Tech 💻

Finally the NotebookLM App

I have been waiting for this! My number one research buddy NotebookLM just got a mobile app - so now you can use all the cool features on your mobile phone.
There are 3 key features that really help:

  • Listen to Audio Overviews offline:
    Once the audio overview is created (you can do it from the mobile as well) you will be able to listen to file offline. So you can walk around in nature without network connection and listen to the conversation about the topic you assembled. Really cool and an enormous time saver if you have many sources and want to get started digging into it.
  • Interact with the hosts:
    To be hones I have not tried this feature yet, and I am not even sure if it is already available for German audio overviews yet.
  • Share to NotebookLM from anywhere:
    This feature comes really handy - imagine you are on the go commuting and you are reading an article or watching a quick YouTube video, then there is an interesting section or piece of information in it that you need in your notebook as source. Easy - you just hit the share button on your mobile and select NotebookLM - it opens and you can select the specific notebook where you want to add the content as a link. It has never been easier to add sources.

#4 Inside AI 🤖

Team Mate

Jeremy Utley, an adjunct professor of Creativity and AI at Stanford University was interviewed on the topic of how AI could power up your creativity.
He basically says you should not treat AI as a tool alone, you should treat it as a team mate - a companion. If you think about it, then it totally makes sense. AI can nowadays remember all of your conversation and give you answers on millions of words, texts and other data in an instant.
Just think of your daily job where you need to do a task you have never done before but you know your experienced colleague know something about it. What do you naturally do? Yes - you go over to your colleagues desk and ask some questions. But you will not ask her for the exact solution because you know that she does not know it. You will have a dialog and both of you will have questions to one another.
If you approach a chat bot - your virtual companion - in the same way then you will be amazed on how much better the answers are and how much better your results you are seeking for are.
One simple first question could always be to ask the AI what information you need to provide in a prompt to get a good answer on a topic. It will tell you it needs - this will also introduce the AI (and chat memory) to the topic you are about to query. It sets the right attention to the model and will set a proper context if you do it properly.

#5 Song of the week 🎶

"Let You Fade" by Linkin Park

About the Artist:
Linkin Park is an American rock band from Agoura Hills, California. Known for their unique blend of rock, metal, and electronic music, Linkin Park gained international fame with their debut album "Hybrid Theory" and has continued to influence the rock genre with albums like "Meteora" and "Minutes to Midnight." The band is renowned for their powerful vocals, innovative sound, and emotionally charged lyrics. Despite the tragic loss of their lead vocalist Chester Bennington in 2017, Linkin Park has continued to create music and tour, maintaining a strong connection with their global fanbase.
Linkin Park reunited with a new lead singer, Emily Armstrong, in September 2024, marking their first major regrouping since the death of Chester. Armstrong, previously known as the frontwoman of Dead Sara, was officially introduced as the new vocalist during a live event in Los Angeles on September 5, 2024. This event was streamed live and featured the debut of their new single, "The Emptiness Machine," with Armstrong performing alongside Mike Shinoda

Background of the Song:
"Let You Fade" was released in 2025 and is part of the deluxe edition of the album "From Zero." The song reflects on themes of memory and loss, with lyrics that express a deep emotional connection and the struggle to hold onto fading memories. It is one of the new tracks added to the deluxe edition, showcasing the band's evolving sound and their ability to convey profound emotions through their music.
