#2 Tech 💻

Refined and Detailed Weekly Blog Workflow

The refined workflow for this blog is broken down into 4 distinct phases. The key here is to understand the chosen topic first and then start with the main drafting and ensuring the AI is used as a tool for executing, not as a replacement for my core understanding.

Phase 1: Ideation & Validation

My goal here is to find a promising idea and ensure it is worth pursuing at the beginning of the week.
Idea Capture:

  • Review my "Second Brain" (Notion) for interesting notes, connections, or open questions.
  • Brainstorm new topics based on recent news, podcasts, industry trends found on e.g. LinkedIn

Topic Validation:

  • Audience Fit: Is this topic genuinely useful or interesting to my target audience? By the way - who is my target audience? Comment below if you like what you are reading.
  • Source Check: Do credible primary sources (scientific papers, official reports, expert interviews) exist? A quick search with Elicit or Perplexity is crucial here.
  • Unique Angle: What is my unique perspective? Can I simplify a complex topic, offer a contrarian view, or provide a practical "how-to" guide?

Phase 2: Research & Structuring

My goal or should I rather say aim, is to become a mini-expert and create a solid blueprint for the article.
Deep Research & Synthesis:

  • Gather 2-3 core sources (e.g., one primary paper, two high-quality articles).
  • Read, ~~highlight, and take notes… ~~well, something I have to admit here is that I am not good at highlighting and taking notes. I rather take the sources and put them in a Google NotebookLM, get audio summaries, mindmaps and ask specific questions that give me condensed blocks of text.
  • This is my "Understand and Condense" step. Focus on extracting key findings, surprising data points, and quotable phrases. Don't just collect links; internalize the information.

Outline Creation:

  • Create a logical structure. A classic format works well:
  • Title: A working title that's engaging and clear.
  • Introduction: The Hook (a question or surprising fact), the Problem, and my Thesis (what the reader will learn).
  • Main Body: 3-5 key points as headings. Under each heading, list the supporting data, examples, or arguments from my research.
  • Conclusion: A summary of the key takeaways and a clear Call to Action (CTA) (will mainly something like "leave a comment").

Phase 3: Content Creation

Here is where I want to bring my articles to life - using AI as an accelerator.
Strategic AI-Assisted Drafting:

  • Instead of asking the AI for a full draft, I will use my detailed outline to guide it section by section.
  • Good Prompts: "Expand on this point from my outline: '\[My heading and bullet points\]'. Write it in a clear and professional tone." or "Explain the concept of \[X\] for a beginner, based on these notes I took: '\[my notes\]'."
  • This gives me a strong "first pass" that is already structured and based on my research.

Authorial Writing & Refinement:

  • This is the most critical step. Rereading the AI-generated text. Rewrite it in my own voice (actually NotebookLM is already pretty good at using my style as it has all my recent blogs as sources and examples).
  • Add personal anecdotes, insights, and opinions.
  • Fact-check every claim and data point generated by the AI against my primary sources.
  • Ensure the flow between sections is smooth and logical.

Visual Asset Creation:

  • Based on my text, identify opportunities for visuals. (Napkin.ai)
  • Graphics: Maybe create charts, diagrams, or infographics to explain complex data (Tools: Canva, Figma). - this would be something new for me to explore if that would become useful.

Phase 4: Publication & Promotion

My goal is to polish the final product and ensure it reaches my audience.
Editing:

  • Proofread: Check for spelling, grammar, and typos (Notion will already do this for my and highlight mistakes). Sometimes I will read the article out loud to catch awkward phrasing.
  • Executive summary: Export the article from Notion and import to Google NotebookLM alternatively use GitHub Copilot and a special prompt with MCP to get the article from Notion and create an executive summary for sharing on social media.

Publish & Distribute:

  • Format the article in Notion for readability using short paragraphs, bold text, and lists.
  • Hit "Publish"!
  • Promotion: Share the article on my social media.

As you can sense the workflow was crafted with the help of AI. Here I used Gemini 2.5 Pro which really surprised my with this nice result. The text above is not a 1:1 copy of the output but almost 80% is. The input was only some bullet points from my initial brainstorming notes.
