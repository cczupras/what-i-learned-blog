# #2 Tech 💻 {color="blue_bg"}
## Zuse Z1 - One of the earliest computers
We went into the “Deutsches Technikmuseum Berlin” mainly because the trip was a gift to our former computer science professor. He keeps inspiring our “old” study group and he always told us about these pioneers in computer science who build the first computers and where thought leaders in the early beginnings of the computer century.
So it was just natural to make a trip to Berlin where there is an exhibition about Konrad Zuse and his Z1 computer. 
It was really amazing to see this “mechanical” computer - yes mechanical - that could crunch some numbers. Unfortunately the rebuild of this computer is not working - yet. Some computer scientists are working on replacing crucial parts.
However it was amazing to see it live. Back in the house where we lived we watched a video about how it works and some parts of it in action.
So inspiring how you would imagine something and then bring it to live.
If you are interested have a look at this “longer” explanation video:
<unknown url="https://app.notion.com/p/1bab04fbcf95800495f1f24a3b880266#1bfb04fbcf958086a2b0fbdf1239e930" alt="bookmark"/>
The Zuse Z1 was one of the earliest computers, designed and built by German engineer Konrad Zuse between 1935 and 1936. Here are some key points about the Zuse Z1:
1. **Mechanical Design**: The Z1 was a mechanical computer, meaning it used mechanical parts like metal strips and pins to perform calculations. It was not electronic.
2. **Binary System**: The Z1 used the binary number system, which is fundamental to modern computing. This was a significant innovation at the time, as many other early computing machines used decimal systems.
3. **Programmable**: The Z1 was programmable, meaning it could execute different instructions based on input. This made it more versatile than simple calculating machines.
4. **Limited Success**: Although the Z1 was a pioneering effort, it was not entirely successful due to issues with reliability. The mechanical parts were prone to errors and did not always function correctly.
5. **Foundation for Future Work**: Despite its limitations, the Z1 laid the groundwork for Zuse's later, more successful computers, such as the Z3, which is considered one of the first fully functional program-controlled computers.
# #3 Sports, Food & Health 🏋️‍♀️ {color="orange_bg"}
## Training Week
This week fortunately was a lot of training. I found enough time to go for a run, an afternoon bike ride a HYROX class and even a strength challenge session with 2 of my best buddies during a meet up weekend.
This week felt great. While running I could feel that it is the beginning of the season and I lost a bit of endurance in the last months. However I think this will come back fast when I start to run more often.
Some sources refer to a phenomenon known as 'muscle memory,' which likely explains why the body adapts to training and makes progress more quickly after a period of reduced training, compared to starting from scratch with no prior training experience.
So it is ok if there are some times where you train less often or with less intensity. But it is important to at come back and find your rhythm in training. It has so many health benefits.
**Keep on training - at least stay active.**

<br>**About the Artist: **
Foo Fighters is a rock band from Seattle, Washington, USA. Known for their energetic live performances and hits like "Everlong," "Learn to Fly," and "Times Like These," Foo Fighters has built a loyal fanbase over the years. Founded by former Nirvana drummer Dave Grohl in 1994, the band has won multiple Grammy Awards and has been inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame.
<br>**Background of the Song:** 
"My Hero" was released in 1997 and is part of the album "The Colour and the Shape." The song is about everyday heroes and is widely believed to be Dave Grohl's tribute to his former Nirvana bandmate Kurt Cobain, though Grohl has stated it's about heroes in general who are ordinary people. The powerful anthem features one of the band's most recognizable drum intros and has become a concert staple.
