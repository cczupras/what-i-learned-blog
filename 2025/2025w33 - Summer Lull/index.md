# #1 Personal ✒️ {color="green_bg"}
## Personal Note
It seems like Tuesday is my new Sunday - where while I am commuting to work write some lines, correct and adjust the prepared texts and publish this blog. 
During summer, newspapers often speak of a 'summer lull' when everyone is on vacation, and new topics are scarce. I find myself in a similar 'summer lull' right now. I've realized I don't have many new topics for this blog; instead, I'm primarily applying what I've learned over the past weeks and months, reflecting on many things, and reviewing them. I'm assembling workflows I've conceived and implementing techniques I've simply picked up.
At the moment, not much new content is emerging because many colleagues are on vacation, leading me to take on a lot of support duties, which leaves little time for learning new things. On weekends, I also have less time to sit at the computer or research technical subjects; if I read, it's often a crime novel, which, while entertaining, doesn't offer much for my daily work or blog content. Plus, there's a lot to do in the garden and around the house—tasks that, while fulfilling, don't typically involve much new learning.
Given this, I've decided to **shorten the blog a bit for the next few weeks**, not covering all areas. It will primarily be a brief overview of what I've learned this week, if anything, and what else I've been up to. This approach, as you know, helps me reflect on my thoughts and let the week pass by. My hope is to still manage publishing on Sundays, though weekends are currently reserved more for the garden due to the beautiful weather.
As always, writing this blog helps me organize my thoughts and **connect the dots**. It's a journey of continuous learning and adaptation, even when new insights aren't immediately apparent.
**Enjoy reading and don’t forget to leave some feedback.**

# #4 Inside AI 🤖 {color="gray_bg"}
## GPT 5
I guess you all have heard meanwhile that openAI has release a new model with GPT 5. From what I heard and read so far it is like expected - the model has been improved - in some areas but has not made a major jump forward. What is a bit different this time is that a lot of invest went into the tooling around chatGPT. Free users also get access to the model with thinking but the tool itself decides when and if needed. Meaning if you ask simple questions the fast model will respond but when you ask more complex questions then the thinking model will kick in.
This is also what I am expecting from all vendors and science that there will be less choice for the normal user - the tools, models and agents will decide which is the appropriate model and tool to use for the task. It gets somewhat autonomous that way - which I like.
A side effect will also be a lot of energy saving because not everybody will use the resource hungry model right away because they think bigger is better.
To be honest I have not played around with the new model yet - and I am not a fan of just playing around with models either. If I have a task to do I will occasionally try different engines and models just to see how I can get the best result for my goal.
