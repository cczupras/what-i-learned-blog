# #2 Tech {color="blue_bg"}
## Prompting 📣
Recently I started not just chatting with different AI’s but also to refine some prompts and also save these in my second brain as reference. This way I am able to reuse them for certain tasks I need to do more often. One example is the new section song of the week - where I want to have the same structure every week. The basics are searched by the AI and I only add a personal note and change some wording.
I have watched a webinar about prompting which was quite inspiring so I share it with you:
<unknown url="https://app.notion.com/p/1b4b04fbcf95801d9e5df3dee0edcf98#1b8b04fbcf9580fdb4a6c5084e794f54" alt="bookmark"/>
In my company I have just joined a working group for AI. This week one of our topics was how we can use prompting to achieve better results. We had a really good discussion and some examples. I also got to know there is a challenge for the prompt of the week - so one that other colleagues could use to help with something in their daily business. I took some great ideas out of the meeting. One I want to share is that you can also use AI to create and refine a prompt with you. Nice idea I think. 
Just tell you favorite AI chat briefly what you want to achieve and ask it to build a prompt for this. Of course this is just a starter but may be it helps starting in that topic.
Another inspiration I got from a podcast (german):
<unknown url="https://app.notion.com/p/1b4b04fbcf95801d9e5df3dee0edcf98#1b8b04fbcf95804fb929eb84e7261a83" alt="bookmark"/>
Basically the idea here was to ask the AI to challenge you - ask you questions and check if you have understood the answer fully that you asked in the beginning.
> "You are here to question and challenge me. No matter what we discuss, phrase your responses in a way that encourages me to think. Come up with meaningful questions whose answers will help me better understand my task or goal. Then, review my answers with me until I am satisfied with the solution.”
**Give it a try - may be share your experience with me - I am always looking for useful prompts.**

This week I also got a Copilot license to explore how we can use it in our company. You can already use the free version in MS Teams. The subscription version also allows you to use predefined prompts and also to create Agents. Guess what - yes I started to build my first agents - at least I tried. I do not quite understand yet how they are working or which benefit they bring in comparison to a saved prompt but I think I will get there.
Well of course I will not share details here but one thing which was helpful and saves a lot of time was to build an agent that operates on our App changelogs. For example I took the changelog and asked the Agent to create me a brief description and some bullet points about the main functionality of the App. That of course needs still some refinements but now I am able to use this agents basically on all our App changelogs and create a nice description and key feature list in no time. And the benefit - they all have the same structure and tonality.

**About the Artist:**<br>Fall Out Boy is an American rock band from Wilmette, Illinois. Known for their catchy melodies and profound lyrics, Fall Out Boy has built a loyal fanbase over the years. The band, consisting of Patrick Stump, Pete Wentz, Joe Trohman, and Andy Hurley, has had numerous hits and released several successful albums.
