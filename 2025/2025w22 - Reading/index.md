## Recipe 🥦
I had some old bread lying around for some days - hard like stones. I hate food waste so I did not throw it away. But what to do with it? Looking in my fridge there was a block of plain Tofu. While I was thinking about what to do a good old German recipe for dumplings (Knödel) came to my mind. Normally done with potatoes and bread. To be honest I was too lazy to cook some potatoes and do all the additional stuff.
But the idea was born and so I thought about how can I change it to a quick recipe - even with using the air fryer instead of cooking? May be I can get crunchy dumplings out of it and add some green stuff as well? 
Yes - I found some frozen broccoli in my fridge and so I began to pulverize the bread in my food processor, added the frozen broccoli as well and pulverized it too. After that I crumbled the Tofu in, added some hot water, oat milk, herbs and spices.
Let the dough calm down for at least 30 minutes so the bread can soak up all the liquid.
Then mold little dumplings or balls and spread a bit of oil onto them.
190 - 200° C for 20 minutes in the air fryer - flip them after 15 minutes and you are done.
Enjoy.

# #4 Inside AI 🤖 {color="gray_bg"}
## What to do with AI ❓
This week I have not done much with AI - yes, I listened to the latest news on my daily and weekly podcasts about the topic but it seems there is so much new stuff coming which is overwhelming.
Looking back at a busy week I had a lot of task but not really something I could think of an AI could do out of the box. Even the code completion by GitHub Copilot which I am experimenting with is not really helping too much in the programming language of AL. But may be it is because I have not set it up properly - something I need to invest some time in.
So my key learning this week is not something specific but rather I need to take a closer look on tasks I do and ask myself - could AI help me here? What does it take to setup a companion or a tool that can do a task for me?
One thing that came to my mind was documenting the things I do - mainly when supporting customers or fixing little issues. Is there a quick way I could just give bullet points or something else to an AI which then takes notes and documents what I have done?
This could be helpful in case I have setup a test system and some weeks later I need to replicate the same setup for the LIVE system as well - we all know how good we can remember details after 4 weeks of other tasks, right?
It could also help with just document functionality I just build, extended or updated.
Is this the right use case for an AI Agent where I can just throw over some bullet points, may be additional data like excel sheets or plain notes as files and then I get a clear and structured output for the documentation or the next status email to a project manager or technical expert?
I think that is worth trying to build - ~~hopefully~~ I will find time this week to try it out and write about the outcome in week 23 of this blog.
Stay tuned - and in case you have a hint for me → go ahead and text me - I will be all ears.
