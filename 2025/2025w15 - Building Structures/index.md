# #2 Tech 👨‍💻 {color="blue_bg"}
## Prompts and XML tags
I found a note about an article from Anthropic in my notes. Reviewing it reminded me what I was doing the last days when prompting. I created a bit more structure for the LLM in the prompts. Sometimes and now even more often I make use of XML tags to group different parts of the prompt.
It really helps to get a better output - not only when using Anthropics Claude models - it also works with Copilot and others.
Here is the link to the original article:
<unknown url="https://app.notion.com/p/1d2b04fbcf9580cdb7d4eedba0909a8a#1d4b04fbcf958039a48ae83258d31179" alt="bookmark"/>
---
To give you an easier example than the ones in the article I will share one of my prompts from this week where I wanted to extract the main points from a presentation I gave on our InfoSpring - a customer conference that we hold every year to show news and meet our customers.
```plain text
Hey Copilot, I want you to wrap up the attached presentation. Follow the given instructions and formatting rules.

<instructions>
- You should extract the 5 key Points with a Keyword and sharp description in one sentence.
- Output should be in english language
</instructions>

<formatting>
- the Keyword should be bold the rest of the text normal
- make use of bullets for the key Points
- add a title on top
</formatting> 

<source document>Application Management - Christian Czupras.pptx</source document>
```
<empty-block/>
The result is the following:
### **Key Points from Application Management - Christian Czupras**
- **Definition**: Application Management encompasses all processes for managing software applications from planning to decommissioning.
- **Goals**: The main goals include maximizing application availability, improving user satisfaction, optimizing implementation processes, and minimizing operational costs.
- **Lifecycle**: The lifecycle includes planning and development, deployment, operation and maintenance, and decommissioning.
- **Business Central**: Regular updates and coordinated planning are essential for maintaining Microsoft Business Central applications.
- **Telemetrie**: Telemetry provides comprehensive event monitoring and detailed insights for optimizing user experience and identifying performance issues.
---
Pretty neat - what do you think? 
As you can see the AI followed exactly my instructions - and really cool about his as well is that it is totally reusable. Just exchange the presentation file and there you go.
Would this be a good idea to create an agent for this and share it with my colleagues so they can directly use it with their Copilot?

# #5 Song of the week 🎶 {color="red_bg"}
## **Sometimes by Gray Daze**
**About the Artist:**
**Gray Daze** is an **alternative rock/grunge** band from **Phoenix, Arizona, United States**. Known for being one of the first bands of Linkin Park vocalist Chester Bennington, Gray Daze has seen several lineup changes and notable events in their history. The band was formed in 1993 and released two full-length albums before disbanding in 1998. They reunited in 2017, and despite Bennington's passing, they released the album "Amends" in 2020, which featured his original vocals.
**Background of the Song:** 
"**Sometimes**" was released in **1994** and is part of the album "**Wake Me**". The song is about the protagonist going through a tough time and feeling unsure about what to do. They feel like everything is falling apart and sometimes they want to leave everyone behind. The song repeats this uncertainty and ends by saying that sometimes things just happen and there's no explanation for it.
