# #1 Personal ✒️ {color="green_bg"}
## Personal Note
**2025** was a year of change for me. I embraced all the challenges and as you have read I have learned a lot. Despite what I have shared here there are also a lot of very personal learnings which all together made me feel more connected and stronger.
**2026** will for sure hold new challenges and I am excited to tackle those. We live in a quite fast moving world where many things are changing, and there is a lot happening in the world. Unfortunately not only positive things.
What I struggled the most with this year was time. Finding enough time for the things I want to do. My mind is constantly producing new ideas, I see things to improve at work, at home and personally. Finding the time to really engage with these things, to think more deeply about concepts or simply finish what I’ve started, can be quite challenging. And that’s not even considering the time needed for quiet moments: creating space for relaxation and regeneration
That is one major thing I want to concentrate and improve in **2026**. For a lot of these challenges I will most likely use AI to help making more out of my time. But there is also a lot you cannot do with AI - resonate with people, take care of relationships, help voluntarily in non-profit projects. This is a balance act which needs a clear mind, structure and courage - at least from my point of view.
One of the projects this year I am most looking forward to is being part of [Team Rynkeby Flensburg](https://www.team-rynkeby.de/die-teams/team-rynkeby-flensburg).
It is a chance to grow with the team, take on those long rides, and contribute to the events. Whether it is securing sponsors or raising funds. **The ride to Paris and the gathering with the other teams from across Europe will mark the culmination of the project—and it will likely be one of the most emotional moments of this journey.** I’m excited to be part of this effort and see what we can achieve together.

# 2025 Review: From Noise to Resonance – My Year of Creation {color="blue_bg"}
### The "Switch": Moving Beyond the Scroll
For a long time, I found myself trapped in the cycle of passive consumption. I was a professional at scrolling, reading, and absorbing, but I lacked the output to match the input. In early 2025, I decided to flip the switch. My mission for the year was simple but challenging: **Stop being just a consumer and start being a creator.**
This year wasn't about adding more "noise" to the world; it was about finding resonance. It was about documenting my "unfiltered learnings" and connecting the dots between technology, physical movement, and the quiet of nature.
### Code & AI: Refining the Second Brain
2025 was the year my "Second Brain" moved from a storage room to an engine room. By leveraging AI-driven workflows, I didn't just work faster; I worked smarter.
- **Documentation as a Habit:** My Notion blog (cczupras.notion.site) became more than just a notebook—it became a public record of my journey. Sharing my technical struggles and breakthroughs in real-time helped me solidify my own knowledge.
- **The AI Synergy:** Instead of fearing the rapid pace of AI, I integrated it into my daily coding routine, using it as a high-level sparring partner that freed up mental space for architecture and creative problem-solving.

### Movement: Finding My Physical Anchor
In a world of abstract code and digital interfaces, I needed something heavy and real. CrossFit and Gravel Biking provided that grounding.
- **CrossFit:** This year, I pushed past mental plateaus I’ve had for years. The box is where I learned that "growth happens at the edge of discomfort"—a lesson that translated directly into my professional life.
- **The Gravel Trails:** Living near the coast and riding through the rural landscapes around Sterup offered a different kind of intensity. Those long hours on the bike weren't just for fitness; they were "active meditation" sessions where my best ideas finally had the space to surface.

### Nature & Growth: Expanding the Horizon
I’ve realized that my best work doesn't happen when I'm glued to a screen. It happens when I disconnect.
- **The Rural Advantage:** Moving away from the urban constant-hum has allowed me to focus on what truly matters. The proximity to nature has been the ultimate filter for the digital overwhelm.
- **Lifelong Learning:** Whether it was a new programming language or a new skill in the garden, 2025 was defined by a beginner's mindset. I embraced the "unfiltered" part of my journey—celebrating the mistakes as much as the successes.
